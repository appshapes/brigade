## Obligations to watch

- **Keep-alive under GitHub's 60-day rule** — scheduled workflows are disabled after 60 days without repository
  activity; a disabled keep-alive lets the free-tier hosted project pause.
- **`TestRunRefusesARunThatOutlivedTheFixtureLease` failed `fast` on the v0.5.0 release commit: ETXTBSY, not
  the lease clock** — run 34537042672 attempt 1 was first read here as a real-clock flake of
  `fixture_lease_test.go` (a 1 s fixture lease against a 2 s sleep). The log says otherwise:
  `describe: spawn failed: fork/exec /tmp/…/lease-adapter: text file busy`. The fixture's first spawn hit
  ETXTBSY (golang/go#22315: `writeScript` wrote the fake with os.WriteFile while a sibling test's fork was in
  flight, and the vfork child held the write fd), and the three stderr assertions failed because describe never
  ran, not because the lease or the 8m0s budget misbehaved. Same mechanism as runs 34232764450 (`cmd/brigade`
  TestScript/inbox), 34355720402 (`adapterkit` two_envelopes) and 34516308749 (`conformance`
  TestExecStdinOpenCatchesAStdinReader). Fixed under ticket 20 by `internal/testutil.WriteExecutable`: every
  executable fixture in the tree is written as a new inode under `syscall.ForkLock`, and `cmd/brigade`'s
  testscript Setup re-materialises the extracted scripts the same way; the conformance and adapterkit fakes are
  since launched as `/bin/sh <script>` (viaShell, childArgv), so nothing in either package execs a fresh file at
  all, and the test's control arm (three spawns inside a 1 s lease, a stopwatch) is gone. Widening the lease
  margin or asserting the numbers instead of the sentence would not have touched it.
- **Claude Code's 50-post mid-turn inbox queue loss** — acked frames silently dropped beyond 50 queued during a
  busy turn; documented known limit of 0.1.0, and the product question (pace/hold posts on the activity flip vs
