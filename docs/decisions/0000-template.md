A file is never Proposed. While a decision waits on someone it is a work item on the Brigade Board tagged
`decision`, written in these same sections; this file is written when it is decided. Delete this paragraph.

## Context

What situation forces a decision. State the constraints as facts, each with its source or its measurement. Two or
three paragraphs at most.

## Decision

What we are doing, in the present tense. The reader should be able to stop here. This section never changes once
the ADR is on `master`.

## Alternatives considered

For each alternative: what it was, and why it lost. An alternative that was never seriously considered does not
belong here. A decision without alternatives is a preference.

## Evidence

The measurements, runs, experiment reports and proofs that informed the choice. A number carries its unit, its
date and what it was measured on. "None measured" is an answer; say why.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to maintain, and what would make us revisit this
decision.

## References

The work item on the Brigade Board that carried the decision or in which it was made ("work item 309"), the
commit, the rule file or page that states the rule, and any outside source.
